[ 04 ] How it works

Three steps.
No sign-up theatre.

DelftRate is built to be used between two lectures, on a phone, with one hand.

01

Find

Search by course, professor or faculty. Every course has one combined score and a short plan for how to study it.

02

Rate

Six sliders from 0 to 10: three about the subject, three about the professor. We combine them for you.

03

Get invited

Your first rating moves the leaderboards and unlocks exclusive invitations from student teams.

[ 05 ] Principles

Anonymous. Numbers only.
About teaching, never the person.

Six criteria and one combined score. There is no comment box, so there is nowhere to get personal.
